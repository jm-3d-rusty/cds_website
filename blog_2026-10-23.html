Linear Agents in IMS-CAD

In the original concept of IMS-CAD of October 2026, agent edges were considered to be generally curvilinear. Subsequently it has been determined that curvilinear edges are not practically required because the 1 nanometer snap grid is 10,000 times smaller than common engineering and manufacturing tolerance of about 1/100th mm. Strictly linear edges are more than sufficient for the IMS-CAD paradigm. This is presented in an addendum to the orginal IMS-CAD article
